# Background music

Optional. Drop one track in here and it gets mixed under the voiceover at
`music.volume_db` (default -22 dB, i.e. well out of the way).

The same licensing rule as b-roll applies: **you need the right to use it.**
Unlicensed music is the single most common reason a Shorts channel gets claimed
or muted. Safe-ish sources are the YouTube Audio Library, Uppbeat, and
royalty-free tracks you have actually bought a licence for.

Supported: `.mp3` `.wav` `.m4a` `.aac` `.ogg`. Tracks are looped to cover the
video, so a 30-second loop is fine.

Set `music.enabled: false` in your config to skip music entirely. Nothing in
this folder is committed to git.
